## Decisions needed before database implementation

- Confirm school levels, class/stream terminology, academic-year and term structure.
- Confirm student numbering, admission-to-student process, and required student/guardian information.
- Agree attendance session/cutoff, late/departure rules, correction authority, outage handling, and staff-attendance scope.
- Provide grading components, weights, boundaries, absence/missing-mark handling, resit/correction process, and report-card requirements.
- Confirm currency and timezone.
- Agree fee, payment allocation, overpayment, refund, receipt, and transaction approval rules.
- Identify required user permissions, reports, privacy/retention rules, and any records to import.

## Decisions that can safely be postponed

- Exact PA/bell hardware and its later integration details, provided it is not needed for initial deployment.
- Optional portal features if the school confirms portals are not part of the first release.
- Optional departments or staff-attendance workflows if they are not needed initially.
- Report layout refinements that do not change which data must be retained.
- Performance optimizations until expected record volumes and real reports are known.
